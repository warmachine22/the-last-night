Field Guide

Mission & Stealth

500 monsters lurk across this fixed forest—half inside 20 large single-floor cabins, half hidden beside trees and brush outdoors. They do not respawn. Stay silent in total darkness to slip past or lose pursuit. Running footsteps, flashlight beams, gunshots, and breaking cabin walls draw them in.

Flashlight & Vital Weak Spots

Monsters are invulnerable in the dark. Shine your limited, upgradable flashlight onto a monster to expose its randomized red vital region (brain, chest heart, or left/right leg). Shoot that glowing red region while the light stays on it to drop the threat. Watch out inside cabins—alerted monsters smash straight through weak interior walls.

Radio Parts & Extraction

Explore the cabins to recover all 6 radio parts, weapons, armor, and flashlight/weapon upgrades. Open your Map & Inventory anytime to check collected parts, discovered cabins, and the Antenna Tower location—then carry the assembled parts to the tower console to escape.
