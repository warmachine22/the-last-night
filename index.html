Field Guide

Flashlight & Muzzle Fire

You carry a fixed, non-upgradable 30s flashlight alongside bright muzzle fire and scarce ammo to spot threats across tight corridors and vast high-ceiling rooms.

Ambush AI & Combat

1–5 zombies hide silently in concealed corners—wait for you to pass a doorway or step inside, then ambush from behind and chase briefly out of their room before returning if you sprint far away. Headshots kill regulars instantly (2–3 body shots); Brutes take 3 headshots or 6 body shots.

Hazards, Ammo & Modes

In Labyrinth Escape, avoid floor hazards and find the single perimeter EXIT. In Fight in Tight Space, hold out wave-by-wave inside a 6-doorway hexagonal arena whose floor cubes shift every 30s while your weak starting flashlight and arsenal upgrade between waves.
